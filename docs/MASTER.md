# Viroqua Business Guide - Design System (NORTHSTAR)

> Re-aligned to the authentic vibe of Viroqua, Wisconsin: The Organic Capital of the Driftless.

## 🌿 Core Philosophy
**The Slow Directory**: Moving away from "tech-platform" aesthetics. This interface should feel like a high-end local almanac or a heritage field guide. It celebrates agriculture, artsy community, and the rugges hills of the Driftless region.

---

## 🎨 Creative Direction

### 1. The Color Story
Inspired by the Viroqua landscape (limestone bluffs, Coulee creeks, and organic fields).

| Tone | Hex/Tailwind | Origin |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Forest Sage** | `#3E5C3D` | The rolling hills of the Coulee region. |
| **Warm Parchment**| `#FDFCFB` | Aged paper, local history. |
| **Burnt Brick**   | `#8B4513` | Historic downtown Viroqua architecture. |
| **Creek Slate**   | `#6B7280` | Local river stones and limestone. |
| **Moss**          | `#E2E8D4` | Organic Valley fields and forest floors. |

### 2. Typography (The "Main Street" Duo)
- **Headings**: `Lora` (Serif). Elegant, grounded, and historic.
- **Body**: `Inter` (Sans). Highly readable, clean, and functional.
- **Micro-Copy**: `Inter` (Uppercase, Wide Tracking). For that "almanac" utility look.

### 3. Visual Vocabulary
- **Paper Layers**: Use very soft shadows and borders that feel like stacked stationery rather than digital "glows."
- **Organic Shapes**: Use slightly deeper border-radii (2rem+) and subtle asymmetrical layouts.
- **Grain & Texture**: Introduce subtle grain overlays to remove the "clinical" digital feel.

---

## 📐 Layout & Content

### 1. Landscape Hero
Instead of a generic tech banner, use a sweeping landscape visual of the Driftless Hills to immediately ground the user in Viroqua.

### 2. The "Community" Grid
Category cards should feel like "sections of a map" or "sketches in a journal."
- **Icons**: Lean into rustic, hand-drawn styles or elegant, thin-line SVGs. No emojis for UI.
- **Stats**: "32 Local Makers" or "12 Main St Shops" — use human language.

---

## 🚫 What we are NOT
- We are NOT a hosting company dashboard.
- We are NOT a SaaS portal.
- We are NOT a typical Silicon Valley directory.
- NO vibrant/synthetic neon colors.
- NO sharp/aggressive sharp corners.
- NO high-tech "glassmorphism" glows.
