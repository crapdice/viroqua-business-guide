# Design Prototypes

This directory contains an interactive prototype to explore different "vibes" for the Viroqua Business Guide.

## How to Review
1. Open `index.html` in your web browser.
2. Use the **floating dropdown** in the bottom-right corner to switch between the 5 design concepts.

## concepts

### 1. Driftless Earth (Default)
**Nature-First.** Serene, rounded, organic. Uses deep greens and creams to reflect the ridges and valleys.

### 2. Main Street Vintage
**Historic.** A homage to the town's National Register architecture. Uses serif fonts, paper textures, and distinctive borders.

### 3. Bohemian Harvest
**Eclectic/Fun.** Reflects the artsy, co-op, and farmers market culture. High contrast, bold typography, warmer colors.

### 4. Nordic Minimalist
**Clean.** Focuses on the "slow living" aspect. Stark black and white, grid-based, massive whitespace.

### 5. Night at the Temple
**Premium/Dark.** A sophisticated evening look inspired by the Temple Theatre. Dark, moody, with gold accents.
